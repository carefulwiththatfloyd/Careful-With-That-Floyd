Published on July 6, 2026

▶ Watch the video on YouTube

Following in Pink Floyd's footsteps in Lille

In this first episode of the concept « A Tour of... », I’ll take you on a journey in the footsteps of the legendary visits made by Pink Floyd and its members to the Lille metropolitan area.

What's on the programme for this guided tour:

  • A glimpse in front of the Zénith de Lille.
  • The location of the former Palais des Sports de la Foire Commerciale de Lille (concert on December 7, 1972).
  • The Stadium Nord Lille Métropole (concert on July 28, 1988).
  • The Decathlon Arena (Stade Pierre-Mauroy), venue for Roger Waters’ solo concerts in 2018 and 2023.
